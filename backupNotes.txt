I belive there are missing pices concrning random. Random itself has an intensity range per track defining the evolution of each tracks random sequence. this can be applied to each tracks parameter by increasing tracks parameters random value (as we did for velocity). the random fonction per tracks parameter can also be moved, locked (to stay independent of tracks global random intensity, meaning no evolution) and can be applied with a slew to make the random sequence more smooth. thus i provided another bank 3 with a variation of these parameters please look at https://docs.torsoelectronics.com/t1/parameter-reference/setup/random-rate again to understand random on the device):
